Overview

Tenant Representation

A lease shapes your costs and your operation for years. Drake represents tenants looking for warehouse, manufacturing and flex space, and he works for the tenant. That means a full look at the market, honest advice about each option, and a firm negotiating position.

He also helps current tenants plan ahead for renewals, expansions and relocations, so there is time to compare options well before a lease ends.

Who it is for

  • Companies leasing their first industrial space
  • Tenants approaching a lease expiration
  • Growing companies that need more room
  • Businesses relocating or consolidating
02

How Drake Works

Process

  1. Needs analysis

    Define size, layout, power, loading, parking, location, budget and move in date.

  2. Market survey

    Identify available spaces and spaces coming available soon, including options not widely advertised.

  3. Tours

    Tour the strongest options and check each one against your requirements.

  4. Proposals and negotiation

    Negotiate rent, term, improvements, operating costs, and renewal and expansion rights.

  5. Lease review and move in

    Work with your attorney on the lease and track the build out through move in.
